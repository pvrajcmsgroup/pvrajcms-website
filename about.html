ABOUT PVRajCMS GROUP

Software, Technology & Business Innovation

PVRajCMS Group is a growing multi-sector business group with a vision to develop software products, technology solutions and business ventures across multiple industries.

Our key areas of interest include Software & Technology, Trading & Financial Technology, Healthcare, Retail, E-Commerce, Hospitality and Digital Services. We aim to turn useful ideas into practical products and services that address real-world needs.

Our Vision

To build PVRajCMS Group into a trusted and diversified brand that brings together technology, financial innovation, healthcare and business opportunities under one organization.

Our Mission

Our mission is to develop useful software, accessible digital solutions and customer-focused business services. We aim to grow through innovation, continuous learning, responsible business practices and a commitment to quality.
